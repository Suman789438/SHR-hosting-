How long does server provisioning take?
All hosting servers, including Free Tier and Custom VPS instances, are deployed automatically right after order verification. Deployment usually completes within 30 seconds.

Yes! Our Starter Free Tier with 500MB RAM and 1GB NVMe storage is 100% free with no hidden charges or required credit card details.

Absolutely. You can seamlessly scale your RAM, CPU cores, and NVMe storage up or down at any time directly from the SHR HOSTER client control panel.
